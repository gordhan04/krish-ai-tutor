## 7. MISCONCEPTION DETECTION EXAMPLES

The engine identifies 4 primary real-world agricultural misconceptions:

### 1. Floating Seeds Misconception (Activity 1.1)
- **Misconception:** *"Floating seeds are healthier and more vigorous because they contain extra air pockets for rapid germination."*
- **Textbook Truth (p. 4):** Damaged seeds are hollowed out by insect pests or fungus, making them lighter so they float. Sound, healthy seeds are dense and sink.
- **Trap Option:** Option B in Question on Activity 1.1.
- **Engine Response:** Flags `floating_is_healthy` misconception, transitions session to `REMEDIATION`, and generates contrastive refutation.

### 2. Fertiliser Superiority Misconception (Table 1.1)
- **Misconception:** *"Chemical fertilisers permanently improve soil texture and provide abundant humus."*
- **Textbook Truth (p. 7):** Fertilisers are inorganic mineral salts providing zero humus. Manure is organic and creates humus-rich, porous soil texture.
- **Trap Option:** Option B in Question on Table 1.1.
- **Engine Response:** Logs `fertiliser_is_superior` in `misconceptions` table, marks `is_remediated=False`.

### 3. Overhead Sprinkler Efficiency Misconception (Fig. 1.5)
- **Misconception:** *"Overhead sprinklers save more water than drip systems in hyper-arid orchards."*
- **Textbook Truth (p. 9):** Drip irrigation delivers water drop-by-drop directly to the root zone with zero surface evaporation, making it the ultimate boon for water-scarce regions.

### 4. Fresh Grain Storage Misconception (p. 11–12)
- **Misconception:** *"Fresh grains retain moisture to germinate faster, so they should be sealed immediately in airtight bags."*
- **Textbook Truth (p. 11–12):** Fresh grains contain moisture that promotes fungal rot, bacteria, and insect infestations; sun-drying is mandatory.
